Research software

Tools and applications

Interactive research systems developed by the Geo-Social Lab for geovisual analytics, spatial interaction, text analytics, regionalization, and geo-social network exploration.

CarSenToGram

CarSenToGram is a geovisual text analytics system for exploring spatiotemporal variation in public discourse, combining social-media text, sentiment, topic modeling, and cartographic representation.

Open CarSenToGram →

Consensus Regions

The Consensus Regions application supports exploration of regional structure and agreement across alternative spatial-network regionalization results.

Open Consensus Regions →

TopicViz

TopicViz supports visual exploration of topics, semantic change, and spatial patterns in communication networks and social sensing data.

Open TopicViz →

FlowMapper

FlowMapper is a web-based framework for designing origin–destination flow maps and communicating movement, migration, and spatial interaction patterns.

Open FlowMapper ↗

Treets

Treets supports interactive exploration of family-tree and geo-social network data, including genealogical connections across people, places, and time.

Open Treets →
